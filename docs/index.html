A FEW THINGS YOU MIGHT BE WONDERING

Good questions.

Can you work with my theme or brand colours?

Yes. Share your mood board, colour palette or brand guidelines in your project brief. We’ll use them as the starting point for the design.

What do you need to prepare a quote?

Your event date, venue, approximate dimensions, budget range and any inspiration images help us understand the scope. It’s okay if some details are still being decided.

How far ahead should I get in touch?

As soon as you have a date or an idea. Timing depends on the design, materials and setup requirements, so include your deadline when preparing your brief.
